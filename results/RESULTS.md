## Caveats

- **Means are only comparable within a dataset.** The 21-sequence and 7-Scenes
  means cover harder/different data than TUM fr1; comparing them across tables,
  or against the fr1 VGGT baseline, is meaningless.
- **Single run per configuration.** The pose fit uses RANSAC and is not
  deterministic; per-sequence differences below ~10% are not established.
- **No VGGT baseline exists for tables 2 and 3.** VGGT-1B does not fit in 6 GB,
  so those rows are DVLT in isolation and say nothing about H1.
- **`FAILED` means no trajectory was produced**, never a score of 0.0.
